## Description

```
GLPenFind lists every UK pharmacy that publishes a price for weight-management pens, and ranks them by what you actually pay — and by how much they ask you to prove first.

WHAT YOU GET
· Prices per pen, for all six doses, read from each pharmacy's own pages
· How many checks stand between you and the order: consultation, photo, ID, GP letter
· Every price links to the page it came from, with the date it was read
· Every listed pharmacy checked against the GPhC register of pharmacy premises

HOW WE RANK
Ease counts only the steps a pharmacy publishes. Where a shop says nothing, we say nothing — it is a floor, not a rating.

WHAT WE DON'T DO
· No discount codes and no special offers. Prescription medicines cannot be promoted on price, and we won't pretend otherwise.
· No paid placements. No pharmacy can buy a higher position.
· Nothing on this app is medical advice, and we do not sell or dispense medicines.

Prices change. Every figure carries the date it was read, and the link takes you to the pharmacy that sets it.
```
