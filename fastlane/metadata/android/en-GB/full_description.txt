GLPenFind is an independent index of what UK pharmacies publish for a private
weight-management consultation, per pen, at every dose.

Every pharmacy in the list is checked against the General Pharmaceutical Council
register of pharmacy premises. Anything we cannot verify is left out rather than
listed with a warning. Each price is read from that pharmacy's own page, and every
line keeps the exact URL and the date it was read, so you can check it yourself.

A home-screen widget shows the cheapest published price at the dose you pick, and
the date it was read — without opening the app, and without a network connection.

Two things are ranked, and neither is for sale:

Price — what the pharmacy publishes today, lowest first.

Ease — what the pharmacy itself says it will ask of you before a prescriber decides:
an online questionnaire, photos, a video call, ID, GP records, and the turnaround it
admits to. Questionnaire-only scores highest; five checks plus repeat photos scores
lowest. It is a floor, not a rating: hide your checks behind a consultation and you
score well here by default, which is exactly why we show the steps next to the score.

Where a pharmacy publishes nothing until you finish its consultation, we say so
instead of guessing.

No accounts. No tracking. No advertising. No analytics. Nothing is stored about you,
because nothing is collected. The app reads a bundled copy of the index and works
without a signal.

GLPenFind is not affiliated with any pharmacy, prescriber or manufacturer, and it is
not medical advice. It is a price list, not a recommendation — whether a treatment is
suitable for you is decided with a prescriber during a consultation, never on price
alone.
