What this is

Registered only Every pharmacy is checked against the General Pharmaceutical Council register of premises. Anything we cannot confirm is left out, not listed with a warning.
Read, not scraped from a rival Prices come from each pharmacy's own page, with the URL and the date kept, so any line can be traced back to its source.
Hassle, counted Questionnaire, photos, video call, ID, GP records — what a pharmacy says it needs, scored, so the shortest path is visible.
No product claims We compare price and process. We do not say which medicine is right for you: that is a conversation with a prescriber.

Before you compare prices

A treatment you would prefer will not be prescribed if it is not suitable for you. Decisions about treatment are made with a prescriber during a consultation, and are never based on price alone. Prescription-only medicines cannot be advertised to the public in the UK, which is why this page describes the service and the process rather than promoting a medicine.

Nothing here is medical advice, and no pharmacy pays to appear. Prices change without warning; the pharmacy's own checkout is the final word.

Where the numbers come from

Read from each pharmacy's own pages by our crawler, with the timestamp stored against every line. Registration is checked against the GPhC register of pharmacy premises.
